Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Applies to the Teleprompter app for Android (package in.sundarprakash.teleprompter) and this website.

Short version: Teleprompter does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and the app does not request internet access. Everything you create stays on your device.

Who we are

Teleprompter is a free, open-source app developed by Sundar Prakash ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, email support@sundarprakash.in.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect or receive any personal or usage data from the app. The app has no user accounts and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software. The app does not declare Android's INTERNET permission, so it cannot transmit data over the network.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage, where other apps cannot read it:

  • Scripts you type, paste or import, including a copy of the original imported text.
  • Settings such as reading mode, speed, font, colours, display profiles, bookmarks and camera preferences.
  • Recordings made in Camera mode. These stay in the app's storage until you choose to save one to your gallery (album "Teleprompter") or share it. A recording you discard is deleted.

You can delete individual scripts in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings › Apps › Teleprompter › Storage, deletes all of the data above. Videos you saved to your gallery remain there until you delete them.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include the app's scripts and settings in your personal backup to your Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone. This backup is controlled by you and handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. We cannot access it.

Permissions

Each permission is requested only when you start the feature that needs it, after the app explains why. You can refuse or revoke any of them and the rest of the app keeps working.

PermissionWhy it's used
CameraShows the live preview and records video in Camera mode. Processed on your device only.
MicrophoneRecords audio with your video in Camera mode. Processed on your device only.
Display over other appsShows the floating script window above other apps when you choose "Float over other apps".
Notifications / foreground serviceShows the required status notification while the floating script window is running.
Storage (Android 9 and older only)Saves a recording to your gallery when you tap Save. Newer Android versions need no storage permission for this.

The app opens files through Android's system file picker, so it only sees the specific file you choose. When you pick a file from a cloud provider such as Google Drive, that provider delivers the file to the app under its own privacy policy.

Sharing

When you use Export or Share, Android's share sheet sends the script or video to the app you choose. What happens to it there is governed by that app's privacy policy. We never receive a copy.

Links to other websites

The app's About screen and this website contain links, for example to Google Play, GitHub and the developer's portfolio. Tapping one opens your browser or the relevant app. Those sites are not operated by this app, and their own privacy policies apply. The app itself does not send any data when you tap a link.

Children

The app is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party trackers, and loads no external fonts or scripts. Like any web host, our hosting provider (Vercel) processes standard technical request data, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver pages and protect against abuse. We do not use this data to identify you.

Security

Your data stays in Android's app sandbox and is protected by your device's security, such as your screen lock and device encryption. Because nothing is transmitted to us, there is no server-side data that could be breached. You can report security issues privately at support@sundarprakash.in.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. All of your data is under your direct control on your device, as described above. If you have questions, contact us and we'll be glad to help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example because a future version adds a feature that handles data differently, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
