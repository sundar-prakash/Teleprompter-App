Getting started

Add a script

On the Home or Library screen, create a new script and type or paste text, or tap Import and choose a file. Supported: .pdf (with selectable text), .docx, .srt, .txt and .md. You'll see a preview before it's saved. The original import is kept, so you can restore it after editing.

Change the speed

Speed is in seconds per line. Tap Slower or Faster in the prompter, or use the slider in settings. A lower number scrolls faster.

Reading modes

Continuous, Single line, Paragraph (with a hold time), Manual and Subtitle. Subtitle mode is available for SRT files imported with Keep subtitle timing switched on.

Troubleshooting

A PDF imports with no text

The PDF is most likely a scanned image. Teleprompter reads the PDF's text layer and doesn't do OCR yet. Copy the text into a document or paste it into the editor instead.

The camera or microphone doesn't work

Go to Android Settings › Apps › Teleprompter › Permissions and allow Camera and Microphone, then reopen Camera mode. Close other apps that might be using the camera.

Where are my recordings?

After recording, tap Save to put the video in your gallery, in an album called Teleprompter. You can also tap Share to send it directly to another app. Recordings you discard are deleted.

The floating window won't appear

Allow "Display over other apps": Android Settings › Apps › Teleprompter › Display over other apps (on some phones: Special app access › Appear on top). On Android 13+, also allow notifications so the floating window's status notification can show.

Picture-in-picture isn't available

PiP needs Android 8.0 or newer, and some manufacturers let you turn it off per app. Check Android Settings › Apps › Teleprompter › Picture-in-picture.

The text is mirrored or upside down

Tap the mirror or flip button in the prompter toolbar to switch back. These are for beam-splitter (glass) teleprompter rigs.

Your data

Everything is stored only on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for details.

  • Delete a script: Library › tap the ⋮ menu on the script › Delete.
  • Delete everything: Android Settings › Apps › Teleprompter › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Move to a new phone: open each script and use Export / share from the prompter menu, or Android's device backup if you have it turned on.

Teleprompter has no accounts, so there is no account to delete.
